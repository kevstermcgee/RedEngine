## The game

**Mechanic:** you spend memories on runes to buy skills but can hold only three, and the exit is always the doorway of whichever far room you have ignored longest, so every trip that earns memories moves the exit.
Core loop (every few seconds): set your sleeping friend down (it jolts them and they stir faster alone), run to a far room for memory beads (hands must be free), come back, step on a rune plinth to buy or sell a skill (Soft Hands, Forgetting, Hush, Anchor, Compass), then lift the friend and carry them to the doorway you have neglected longest, before the house crumbles from the edges inward (outer ring 40 s, middle ring 62 s, core 82 s, on a visible clock) and before the stirring meter hits 100. Win: three rooms delivered. Lose: friend wakes, or you fall.
**Adapted/cut:** ten rooms became three rounds in one house (each round starts with a shorter clock and a different neglected door); the 'exit drift' is a var-chosen one of four fixed doorways with a beacon (Compass skill) because top-level objects cannot move; 'without waking' is time and set-downs, not noise, because rules cannot read player speed.

The mechanic is what the player does most: running between far rooms and the plinths, deciding what to hold.
