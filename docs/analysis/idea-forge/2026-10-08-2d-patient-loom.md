- The card: YOU juggle three timers; each one runs a different law of the room, BUT thrown objects remember where they came from and return when thrown again. Pushback: Every success wakes something bigger. Goal: Gather five parts of a machine and assemble them in the right order.

## The game

**Mechanic:** you keep three decaying timers alive by standing on their dials (GLIDE: throws fly fast, else crawl; GATE: the machine accepts parts only while open; LULL: wardens sleep only while it runs), and every throw can be undone by throwing again, which sends the last thrown part back to where it started. **Loop:** run between three pads and the part row, throw the next part (in the order printed at the bottom) over the wall into the machine while GLIDE and GATE are up, recall strays or wrong-order parts, never let LULL hit zero. **Win:** five parts accepted in order (best time saved via `persist`). **Lose:** a warden touches you. **Pushback:** each success raises LULL's drain and the warden that wakes is bigger and faster (3 tiers). **Adapted/cut:** 'thrown again' = the action key with no part in reach recalls the last thrown part home (home hardcoded per part); throws are east-only (no aiming); no homework story dressing.
