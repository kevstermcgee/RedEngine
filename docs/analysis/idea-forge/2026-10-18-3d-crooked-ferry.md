## The game

**Mechanic:** you lay a tool across a gap and whistle (swing), and an echo repeats that placement at the next gap a beat later, while every tool you use wears into a smaller one (oar, plank, peg).

Core loop: pick up the tool (E), carry it to a gap's edge, set it down, cross, then whistle at the right moment so the echo copies the biggest tool you just used at the next gap; the smaller tool that is left must do the rest. Gaps need oar, oar, plank, plank, peg reach, so whistling after the oar and after the plank solves it. Ink slabs rise every 8 s and deactivate every pier piece and span they pass, so you must keep climbing. Win: reach the statue's plinth on the top ledge. Lose: fall into the ink (touch the ground), or be erased.

Adapted/cut: 'copies your last move' is the last tool placement, not any movement (rules cannot see movement). The whistle is the bat swing. Tools wear on placement into the next tool, not by repeated use. 'Never going down' is enforced by the ink and the fall, not by forbidding stepping back to a lower ledge. The echo is a ghost statue plus a span, with no sound of its own. Level layout and rules are generated by a python script kept outside the repo (/tmp), so the JSON is the source of truth.

## Idea fit

Expressed: tools that wear into other tools (three props swapped by `place`), a rising ink that erases (stacked slabs plus `deactivate`), reaching the highest point, a delayed echo (countdown var ticked by `every`). Not expressed: echoing the player's actual movement, whistling as an input, a sound for the echo. Missing: a rule-readable use/whistle input and the player's position, a sound action, per-entity timers (worked around with one global countdown).
