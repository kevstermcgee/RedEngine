# What building three physics games taught us about Red (2026-09-29)

Three small physics games were built on Red as pure data, headless, without touching the engine: **Knockdown Alley** (throw crates and swing a bat at pedestal stacks over a pit), **Domino Halls** (three chain-reaction rooms started by one swing) and **Fling Delivery** (throw a parcel across gaps). Their agents wrote detailed feedback with repros and measured tables (`FEEDBACK_PROMPT.md` in each project); `D:\ClaudePlayground\RED_ENGINE_IMPROVEMENT_PROMPT.md` merged and ranked it into five work items. Every claim was treated as a hypothesis and reproduced with a failing test before it was fixed; this note says what changed, what proves it, and what was deliberately left out.

## The short version

* `replay` really did apply every bat strike and rule `impulse` twice (item 1). The live simulation was deterministic; only the replay was wrong. One line of design fixed it: a trace records causes (inputs, external pushes), and a replay re-derives outputs.
* Rules, `sim` expectations and the client dump can now see loose props (item 2): "score = props in the pit" and "the bell fell" are rules; the two games' Python proofs run as plain `checks.sim` entries on their unchanged maps.
* A thrown prop leaves the hand the same way offline and on the server, with the player's own velocity, a scene-authored `throw_speed`, an upward component, a short holder-ignores-it window and a hold pose that respects low walls (item 3). The real client and the sim release a crate to the same centimetre.
* The verification tools honour the map (item 4): a spawn on a deck, the scene's tuning and jump pads in walks, a `start` rule that opens a gate, a blueprint whose spawns live in its `scene` block, and the offline client starting where a match would.
* A dozen small frictions became data or documentation (item 5): `look_at` aiming, zone merging, hidden `_` vars, `info` saying whether a thing is loose and how heavy it is, `checks.lint.ignore`, and the measured edge orders and topple directions written down.

Each item is one commit with its ADR, and `scripts/ci.sh` was green in both feature sets before each commit (one load-sensitive real-UDP test, `net_sim`'s cruel-link case, failed once in a full run and passed on every isolated and re-run stage; it is unrelated to these changes).

## The physics facts, now encoded

`describe physics` documents `release_velocity`, `release_grace_ticks`, `hold_pose` and `topple_direction` (front = local +Z; yaw Y falls toward (-sin Y, -cos Y); Domino Halls' spacing and corner recipe); `info` prints the mass every game had to read from `PROP_DENSITY` (the crate is 21 kg, as Knockdown Alley measured). The bat's `6 * min(mass, 4)` N*s cap, `PROP_GRAVITY` 14, reach 2.2 / 2.3 m and windup 0.09 s are unchanged and the fixtures depend on them (`strike_replay.json` needs a 0.6-scale crate to fly; the domino line needs yaw -90 to fall along +X).

## What the games can now remove (not edited: they are their owners' projects)

* **Knockdown Alley**: the CLAIM phase and the `pit` var (`{prop_enter: {zone: pit_near}}` -> `add score`, or `props_in(pit_far)`); `scripts/prove_knockdown.py` (its `demo_knock` proof is one `checks.sim` entry with `{prop: far_crate_a, y_lt: 1}` / `in_zone`); the dummy blueprint `lint` spawn and the pit-floor spawn plus lift pad (a deck spawn lints and walks now); `lint_ignore` on the deck (`checks.lint.ignore: ["drop"]`); `PIT`/`LOADED` on the HUD (`_pit`, `_loaded`).
* **Domino Halls**: the carry-the-bell win (`{prop_below: [bell_1, 0.5]}` or `tilt(r1_d2) > 60` rules); `scripts/cascade_check.py` (room 1 passes as a `checks.sim` entry with `tilt_gt` / `y_lt`); `author.py`'s `grab()` and swing aiming (`look_at`); the `box` volumes used instead of zones (`scene.zones` merge now); `replay` as the determinism proof (it is clean for strikes now).
* **Fling Delivery**: player-side delivery detection (`{prop_enter: {zone: goal}, prop: package}`); the no-pit design (`{prop_below: [package, y]}` -> `reset`); `scripts/measure_sim_throws.py` (`{prop: package, near: [...]}` and the report's prop poses); the always-open footbridges (a `start` rule gate is open to lint) and the walkable strip past the canal (a `checks.walk` over the pad passes; lint's flood-fill still does not); `t_start`/`started` on the HUD (`_t_start`); its `tools/throwlab` table is superseded by `tests/fixtures/throw.json`.
